Built For Speed

Racing & Motorsports Culture

Racing apparel has its own visual language: speed, aggression, and a logo that still has to read at highway speed from the rear window of a truck. This is the world I’ve spent the most time in.

Built For Pride

Team, School & Organization Spirit

Spirit wear lives or dies on identity: a mascot, a mark, a color combination people are proud to put their name under. This is where I get to build that from the ground up.

Built On Request

Businesses, Organizations, & Events

Not everything is a logo system. Some of this is workwear for trade companies, some of it is small personal or memorial runs, the kind of jobs that don’t come with a brief so much as a conversation.

Print Practices

Separations, Every Time.

I break every design down into its own color separations before it ever reaches a screen, and I know what a press actually needs to hold up: tight registration, a color count the run can handle, detail that survives instead of turning to mud. In over a decade of both personally printing and handing off finished art, nothing I’ve created was ever unprintable.

Twelve-Plus Years, One Print Shop

Crisp & Son

Before any of this was a portfolio section, it was a working relationship. Over twelve years running designs through Crisp & Son, a long-term print partner I’ve grown alongside as both of us got better at the job. You can see the brand’s evolution in the work itself: what changed, what stuck, and what I’d still stand behind today.

Still Love A Well-Designed T-Shirt

Every Shirt Is Still A Design Problem.

Apparel is where my design foundation was built. Long before I had a title, I was solving the same problem this whole site is built around: what does this group of people actually want to say, and what’s the simplest, strongest way to say it. Fourteen years and a lot of other kinds of work later, I still take these jobs for the love of it. There’s something honest about a design that has to survive a press, a wash, and a few years of wear before anyone judges whether it worked.
