Business ImpactGenerated an estimated $100,000 in business value by strengthening company culture through memorable, story-driven communication of ACA’s Guiding Principles.

The Goal

Bring the values the company holds dear to life.

I was tasked to create a powerful visual story that would introduce and embed ACA’s Guiding Principles into the hearts of every employee. More than just a message, the film needed to inspire, connect, and reinforce the culture that drives ACA forward.

Direction and Leadership

I led the vision for the film from the initial concept through final delivery. Working alongside internal stakeholders, I shaped the story, tone, and message to ensure the final piece felt authentic, inspiring, and true to ACA’s culture.

Scriptwriting and Storyboarding

I wrote the script to bring each principle to life through real stories and relatable moments. Storyboards were crafted to plan every scene with intention, ensuring a clear narrative flow and an emotional connection from start to finish.

Art Direction

From set design to cinematography and color grading, every visual decision was made to elevate the story. The look and feel were designed to be cinematic, warm, and human — reflecting the people and purpose at the core of ACA.

The Most Important Part: Collaboration

This project was a true team effort. From internal partners to the production crew, collaboration made it possible to create something meaningful that employees could see themselves in and be inspired by.

The Impact

A training experience that informs, engages, and scales.
