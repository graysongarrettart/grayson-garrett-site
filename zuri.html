ZURI UNSHAKEN

Amid soft blue fog and muted ground, Zuri stands firm — one arm raised, gripping a massive axe in a clear show of determination and unshakable courage before the coming battle.

ObjectiveExplore sci-fi armor design grounded in wear, history, and quiet defiance.

The Thought Process

Research shaped both the story and the design. I explored sci-fi lore, drawing from classic armor silhouettes and giving them a worn, realistic edge. The white armor, streaked with rust, reflects the passage of time and the weight of battles fought — a visual marker of the character’s journey and resilience.
