ObjectiveDeveloped a cohesive visual system that strengthened the restaurant’s market presence, improved brand consistency, and created a memorable customer experience across every interaction.

The Brief

The client wanted more than just another logo.

Kabob Kingdom needed an identity that felt authentic to both its Mediterranean roots and its home in Greenville, South Carolina. The goal wasn’t simply to create a recognizable logo, but to develop a visual system that could tell a story before the first bite.

The Solution

Build a brand rooted in the physical place in which it resides.

Every design decision pulled inspiration from Greenville itself. Local landmarks became recognizable symbols, typography reinforced confidence and clarity, and the visual system was designed to scale naturally across menus, signage, vehicles, and merchandise.

The palette draws from both Kabob Kingdom’s Mediterranean heritage and the character of Greenville. Rich blues echo the Reedy River and Liberty Bridge, while neutral grays provide balance and versatility. Together, the colors create an identity that feels bold, welcoming, and unmistakably local.

Food Truck Mockup

The Menu

Designed for quick decision-making.

For the menu design, I focused on clarity over decoration. A clean layout always beats a busy one, especially when customers are trying to make a quick decision. I kept the structure straightforward so people could find what they’re looking for without scanning through clutter. Simple hierarchy, legible type, and smart spacing make the whole thing feel easy to use and quick to read.
