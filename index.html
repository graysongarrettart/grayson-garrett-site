SOME IDEAS NEED MOTION.

What are we actually trying to say?

Before a storyboard or a single scouted location, I get to the bottom of what needs to be said — not how it should look, but what truth it has to carry. That’s how pacing, tone, and the space between beats get built with intention instead of guesswork.

Video becomes the tool once the message is clear.

SOME IDEAS NEED DESIGN.

What do we need to communicate?

Before Illustrator or InDesign ever opens, I ask why the message matters and who actually needs to hear it. Every type choice, color, and unit of space is doing work toward that answer — contrast and hierarchy in service of clarity, not decoration.

Using design isn’t the goal. It’s the solution.

SOME IDEAS NEED CONCEPTS.

What story does this tell?

Before the stylus touches the tablet, I ask what world this character or place actually belongs to. Every silhouette, texture, and gesture is chosen to solve a story problem before anyone has to ask the question out loud.

Concept art solves questions before they’re asked.

SOME IDEAS NEED…

You need someone who can meaningfully contribute to every part of the conversation. I’ve sat in rooms where the conversation moves from story arcs to shot lists, then into typography and UI logic — sometimes in the same hour. I don’t just keep up. I contribute.
