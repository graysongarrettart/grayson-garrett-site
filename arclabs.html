The Challenge

A growing brand spread across 7 campuses needed a clear visual system.

ArcLabs was expanding faster than its materials could keep up with. Different campuses, different audiences, and years of ad hoc requests had left the brand without a consistent voice, making the school harder to recognize and easier to overlook.

The Solution

A cohesive art direction, built over time, that unified the message.

Rather than a single rebrand delivered all at once, the system grew piece by piece, campaign by campaign. Each new deliverable reinforced the last, so the brand could flex across audiences and formats without ever losing what made it recognizably ArcLabs.

Partnership Beginnings

Understanding the Problem. Applying the Solution.

The first assignment wasn’t about reinventing the brand. It was about stepping in, understanding the business quickly, and solving an immediate need. I needed to learn the audience, work within what already existed, and deliver something purposeful. More importantly, I wanted to establish the kind of partnership I believe in: one where the creative isn’t separated from the strategy, and the mission behind the work matters as much as the work itself.

Gaining Creative Trust

From Execution to Direction.

With a better understanding of the business came more room to lead. These direct-mail campaigns gave me the opportunity to move beyond executing a defined need and begin shaping how ArcLabs spoke to its audience. I developed the visual direction and punchier messaging around two very different moments in the student journey, turning business objectives into creative built to get attention and drive action.

Turning Interest Back Into Action.

The next challenge sat at the opposite end of the funnel: prospective students who had already shown interest but never enrolled. I built the campaign around that existing intent, pairing direct, motivational copy with a more energetic visual language designed to reconnect with the reason they reached out in the first place and give them a clear path back in.

Make Enrollment Feel Like a Milestone.

For newly enrolled students, the goal wasn’t simply to label a package. It was to make receiving it feel like an accomplishment. The mailer transformed a functional touchpoint into a branded welcome moment, building excitement and reinforcing the decision a student had just made.

Supporting Student Success

Turning a Reward Into a Reason for Success.

The next challenge moved beyond recruitment. ArcLabs needed graduates to stay connected and report their employment after completing the program, with a popular branded hoodie serving as the incentive. I turned that existing program into a focused promotional campaign, using the hoodie as the hero and building the message around accomplishment rather than administration. A campus poster created awareness in person, while a companion email carried the same visual language and message directly to graduates.

Extending the Brand

Familiar DNA. A Different Audience.

As the partnership expanded, so did the brand challenge. ArcLabs ATI Services needed to feel connected to the Welding School while serving a very different audience focused on testing, inspection, training, and code compliance. I carried forward recognizable elements of the established ArcLabs identity, then adjusted the hierarchy, imagery, and visual language around a more technical, professional message. The result was a sister brand that felt related without feeling interchangeable.

At this point, the work became less about solving individual projects and more about building a visual system that could grow alongside ArcLabs.

By this point, the work had moved beyond solving one-off creative needs. ArcLabs needed its growing library of collateral to feel like it came from the same place, even when the audience, message, and format changed. The goal became bigger: take the visual language we had been developing together and turn it into a flexible system that could scale.

The First Touchpoint

Give Them a Reason to Keep Reading.

Designed as first-contact collateral for prospective students, the sell sheet had to communicate the essentials quickly: who ArcLabs is, what students can learn, and where that training can take them. The challenge was finding the right balance between information and energy without overwhelming someone encountering the school for the first time.

The Flagship Piece

Building the System’s Anchor.

The Program Guide took everything we had learned and gave it a home. Built as a substantial, large-format trifold, it became the anchor of the new print system. An integrated pocket housed the accompanying sell sheet and supporting materials, allowing each piece to stand on its own while working together as one cohesive recruitment experience.

Same Brand. Different Conversation.

From Recruiting Students to Recruiting Their Employers.

Training a welder is only half the job. ArcLabs is just as invested in what happens after graduation, helping students turn their training into real employment. The Student Services piece shifts the conversation from education to opportunity, connecting industry partners with trained, job-ready welders while showing the support ArcLabs provides beyond the classroom.

Brand IRL.

Something Worth Wearing.

The ArcLabs identity also needed to live beyond recruitment materials. Across instructor apparel, student incentives, and milestone pieces, I translated the visual language into designs people could actually wear with pride. Each piece served a different purpose, from distinguishing instructors to celebrating student achievements, while reinforcing the culture and sense of belonging behind the brand.
