SPYDR PMC CONCEPT ART

SPYDR is a fictional PMC designed to showcase skills in military design, focusing on both aesthetics and real-world functionality.

ObjectiveDemonstrate military design skill through tactical gear variation, balancing real-world functionality with a unified group identity.

The Characters

This concept art for SPYDR showcases four different character designs, each representing a unique style of tactical gear and attire. The variations emphasize the blend of practicality and character, reflecting different roles or enemy types within the fictional PMC. Each design highlights distinct layers of equipment, from modern military essentials to rugged, more specialized outfits. The goal was to explore a range of looks while maintaining the group’s unified identity.
