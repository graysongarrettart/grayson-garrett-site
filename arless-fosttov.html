ARLESS FOSTTOV KNIGHT OF DORIAN IV

Viewing this piece transports you to a world where honor and courage dominate, reminiscent of classic Napoleonic war paintings. It depicts a war hero in a grand, heroic manner, blending the timeless allure of the warrior spirit with the enduring legacy of those who boldly confront the darkness.

ObjectiveBlend classic heroic painting tradition with sci-fi world-building to portray a warrior’s legacy.

The Thought Process

In this painting, I used teal and deep blue to create an otherworldly feel, evoking the vastness and mystery of space around Dorian IV. The colors not only highlight Arless’s armor and sword but also heighten the sense of futuristic wonder and intrigue. My aim was to immerse viewers in a world where everything feels both distant and intriguing.
