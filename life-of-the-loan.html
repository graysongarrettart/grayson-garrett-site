The Challenge

New hires didn’t have a clear picture of how ACA really works.

Onboarding relied on one-on-one explanations from team leads across departments. It worked, but it was inconsistent, time-consuming, and left gaps in understanding.

Success Looked Like

A repeatable, engaging way to show the entire loan lifecycle — one that builds confidence, removes silos, and helps new employees see how their role impacts the big picture.

Finding the Visual Language

Once we understood the big picture, we needed a visual style that felt modern, welcoming, and distinct. The video game / comic inspired direction gave us flexibility to simplify complex ideas while keeping the focus on the people behind the process.

Crafting the Story

The characters were designed to reflect the environment new hires step into at ACA, which is very much collaborative, diverse, and people-driven. The cast includes a wide range of backgrounds and personalities, intentionally crafted to mirror the kind of team members employees will actually work alongside.

Character Selection Screen Concepts

Doing the Research

Before I even started sketching out the characters, I partnered with various department heads across the company to understand how each team fit into the business and the overall loan lifecycle. Those conversations shaped the script and voice of each episode. I use storyboards early in narrative discussions with stakeholders to give them a visual sense of what to expect.

Animating the Episode

Once the script and visual language were approved, I transitioned to After Effects to bring the compositions to life. Each segment was built to flow like a video game cinematic, reminiscent of older titles like Sly Cooper™. This allowed us to push the story forward while keeping the tone energetic and professional.
