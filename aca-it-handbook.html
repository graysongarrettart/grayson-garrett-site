ACA IT HANDBOOK

At American Credit Acceptance, I led the creation of a centralized IT handbook by gathering years of legacy knowledge and transforming scattered documentation into one cohesive, intuitive resource. The result was a scalable knowledge system with an estimated business value exceeding $1 million.

Business ImpactA centralized knowledge system that delivered an estimated $1 million in business value by preserving institutional knowledge and improving access to critical information.

The Problem

Critical knowledge scattered everywhere.

Years of IT processes, policies, and resources were spread across minds, documents, and long-lost inboxes. Information was hard to find, inconsistent, and time-consuming to maintain.

The Solution

One handbook with all the answers.

I helped consolidate all of this legacy knowledge into a single, structured handbook. Information architecture and visual design work in harmony to make content easy to find and understand.

Content First. Design Second.

We lead with content because clarity solves more problems than decoration ever will. In IT, we saw confusion grow when processes and expectations were not clearly documented. By focusing on the message first, we set a standard from day one so there is no doubt about what needs to be done or how. The design then becomes a tool to guide people through that information.

The design of the cover was a homage to the SLIC wall within the IT collab space.

One Playbook For All

Let’s take the guesswork out of the expectations.

Clarity Over Clutter

Every element on a page should earn it’s place.

Eventually dubbed, “The Things That Matter,” this handbook sets the tone for success in IT.

By being thoughtful about what we say and how we say it, we broke conventions and helped our team tackle challenges with clarity.
